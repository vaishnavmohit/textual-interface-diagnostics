# T1 results — statistics for the submission gate

Generated by `analysis/t1_statistics.py` from the committed source files.
Reproduce: `python analysis/t1_statistics.py`.

Inputs: 36,418 Bongard-OW · 4,172 Bongard-HOI · 7,990 Winoground valid
predictions. Invalid outputs are dropped pairs throughout, never scored as
errors.

---

## 1. Clustering was measured, not assumed

Bongard-OW's 500 queries come from 251 support sets (one positive, one negative
each), so they are not *a priori* independent. Measured across 83 runs:
**median ICC +0.015, max +0.391**. Cluster-bootstrap CIs are therefore close to
naive binomial ones.

*Reporting line:* "Confidence intervals resample support sets (uids) rather than
queries. The measured intra-cluster correlation is negligible (median 0.015), so
this choice is conservative rather than consequential." That answers the
paired-design question with a number instead of an assurance. Note the max of
0.39 — a single run does show real clustering, so the correction is not vacuous.

## 2. Model rankings do not survive their confidence intervals

**Only 2 of 61 adjacent orderings are separable at 95%.**

For the CA / own-descriptions block the defensible structure is three bands:

| Band | Models |
|---|---|
| ~87–94% | Gemini 2.0, GPT-4o, Gemma3-27B, Pixtral-12B |
| ~66% | LLaVA-7B |
| ~48–59% | Gemma3-12B, Llama-Vision-90B, Gemma3-4B, LLaVA-Llama3, Llama-Vision-11B, LLaVA-13B |

**Action:** present open-weight results as bands, not a ranked list. Drop any
"model A beats model B" claim between adjacent rows. Two models in the bottom
band are degenerate (§5), so that band is partly non-discrimination rather than
graded ability.

## 3. The paradigm ordering holds — via direction, not per-cell significance

| Family | Contrasts | Direction | Sign test | Individually sig. (Holm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 paradigm (OW) | 16 | 12 of 16 favour the ladder | p = 0.077 | **11/16** |
| F5 paradigm (HOI) | 26 | **24 of 26** | **p = 0.00001** | 2/26 |

On Bongard-OW the ladder (CA > DRL > DVRL) is significant cell-by-cell: e.g.
GPT-4o CA > DRL (Δ = 4.8, Holm p = 0.024), Gemini CA > DVRL (Δ = 11.4, p < 0.001),
GPT-4o DRL > DVRL (Δ = 7.1, p = 0.003).

On Bongard-HOI only 2 of 26 survive Holm — n = 100 per split is simply
underpowered. But 24 of 26 point the same way, which a sign test rejects at
p = 0.00001. **Report both**: the per-cell tests are inconclusive on HOI; the
consistency across splits and models is not. Claiming HOI supports the ordering
on per-cell significance alone would be wrong; claiming it fails to support it
would also be wrong.

## 4. The perception swap: consistent direction, individually underpowered

Seven reasoners ran over both Pixtral and GPT-4o descriptions on the **same 137
items**. GPT-4o descriptions win in **7 of 7** cases (median Δ = +5.1 points),
but only one survives Holm:

| Reasoner | Δ (GPT-4o − Pixtral) | 95% CI | Holm p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen2.5-14B | +7.3 | [+2.9, +12.1] | **0.044** |
| LLaVA-34B | +11.7 | [+3.7, +19.4] | 0.061 |
| DeepSeek-R1-14B | +5.1 | [−0.7, +11.4] | 0.757 |
| Phi-3-14B | +5.1 | [−1.4, +11.4] | 0.757 |
| Phi-4-14B | +4.4 | [−0.7, +9.5] | 0.730 |
| Llama-Vision-11B | +4.4 | [−1.5, +10.6] | 0.757 |
| LLaVA-7B | +5.1 | [−2.1, +12.0] | 0.757 |

Sign test on the direction: **p = 0.016**.

*Reporting line:* the description source matters, and its effect is consistent
across independent reasoners, but n = 137 leaves individual comparisons
underpowered. State the effect at the family level with the sign test, and
report the per-reasoner CIs rather than asserting each one. **Do not claim a
per-model perception effect.**

## 5. ICA on Winoground: significant, and now with a mechanism

All **6 of 6** CA→ICA contrasts survive Holm, with perception and reasoner held
fixed. Better, the paired tables give the correction/harm decomposition (plan
item T2.3) directly:

| Metric | Δ | 95% CI | Corrections | Harms | Ratio | Holm p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Image | +10.0 | [+5.8, +14.5] | 63 | 23 | 2.7 : 1 | 0.0002 |
| Group | +8.0 | [+4.0, +12.2] | 53 | 21 | 2.5 : 1 | 0.0016 |
| Text | +7.0 | [+3.2, +11.0] | 51 | 23 | 2.2 : 1 | 0.0034 |

Re-inspection is not a uniform improvement: it **breaks 21–28 items it had
already answered correctly**, while fixing 51–63. The net gain is the residue of
a much larger two-way churn. That is a far more informative claim than "+8.0
points", and it is what E3 asks for to license calling ICA adaptive.

## 6. Degeneracy screen

`t1_accuracy_ci.csv` carries `answered_positive_pct` and a `degenerate` flag for
every run. On a balanced 50/50 benchmark an accuracy near 50% means something
different for a model answering one class 90% of the time. Two open-weight CA
runs are flagged; their accuracies should never be quoted without it.
